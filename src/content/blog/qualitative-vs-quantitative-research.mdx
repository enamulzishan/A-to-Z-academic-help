## The Core Differences

At the most basic level, quantitative research deals with numbers and statistics, while qualitative research deals with words and meanings.

### Quantitative Research
Quantitative research is used to quantify the problem by way of generating numerical data or data that can be transformed into usable statistics. 

**Common methods:**
- Surveys with closed-ended questions
- Experiments
- Observations recorded as numbers

### Qualitative Research
Qualitative research is primarily exploratory research. It is used to gain an understanding of underlying reasons, opinions, and motivations.

**Common methods:**
- Focus groups
- Individual interviews
- Ethnography / Participant observation

## Which Should You Choose?

Your choice of methodology should be driven purely by your **Research Question**. 

- If you want to know "How many?" or "What is the relationship between X and Y?", use a quantitative approach.
- If you want to know "Why?" or "How does an individual experience X?", use a qualitative approach.
