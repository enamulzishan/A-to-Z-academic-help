## What is a Literature Review?

A literature review is a critical evaluation of existing research on a particular topic. It demonstrates your knowledge of the field, establishes the context for your own research, and identifies the gaps your study will fill.

## Stop Summarizing, Start Synthesizing

The most common mistake students make is writing a "laundry list" of summaries:
- *Author A said X.*
- *Author B said Y.*
- *Author C said Z.*

Instead, you must **synthesize**:
- *While Authors A and B agree that X causes Y, Author C argues that Z is the true catalyst.*

## Structuring the Review

### Chronological
Trace the development of a concept over time.

### Thematic
Organize the literature around key themes, debates, or methodological approaches. This is usually the strongest approach for a dissertation.

### Methodological
Focus on the different methods researchers have used to study the problem.
