## 1. Identify Your Core Interests

The first step in choosing a PhD topic is to reflect on what genuinely interests you. A PhD is a 3-4 year commitment (often longer), and you will be spending thousands of hours reading, writing, and thinking about this single subject.

## 2. Read Broadly, Then Narrow Down

Start by reading recent review articles in your field. These papers often explicitly state what is currently unknown (the "research gaps"). 

- Look for phrases like "Future research should explore..."
- Pay attention to contradictory findings in the literature.

## 3. Consider Feasibility

A great idea is useless if it cannot be executed. You must consider:
- Do you have access to the necessary data or participants?
- Is there sufficient funding or equipment available?
- Can this be completed within the standard PhD timeframe?

## 4. Discuss with Potential Supervisors

Once you have 2-3 solid ideas, arrange meetings with potential supervisors. Their experience can quickly help you determine if an idea is viable, novel, and aligned with their own research interests.
